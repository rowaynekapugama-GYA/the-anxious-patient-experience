How to

When a booking request comes in

  1. You get an email at the APE inbox, and the request shows under Bookings as "To confirm". The time is already held, so nobody else can book it online.
  2. Block the time in Core Practice and enter the patient.
  3. Reply to the patient to confirm and send the new patient information pack.
  4. Press Mark confirmed. When the deposit arrives, press Deposit received.

If a patient cancels

Press Cancel booking. The time opens up on the website again. If it was an opening special time, the $250 price comes back for that time too.

Blocking days when you are away

  1. Go to Availability.
  2. Under Block time off, choose the first day you are away. For more than one week, also choose the last day.
  3. Add a note if you like (patients never see it) and press Block these days.
  4. Those days now show as blocked in the list, and patients cannot choose them.
  5. To block one time only, tap that time in the list. Tap it again to open it.
  6. To open a whole day again, press Open this day next to it.

Blocking a day does not cancel requests already on it. If there are any, the dashboard tells you so you can contact those patients.
