Your Guide to a Calmer Appointment

Thank you for booking your first visit at The Cronulla Dentists. We’re looking forward to meeting you. Here’s a little about what to expect before you come in and on the day.

You’re not alone

If you feel anxious about visiting the dentist, please know that you are far from alone. Dental anxiety affects people of all ages and backgrounds.

Your anxiety is real, and it makes sense. Whether it comes from a past experience, a fear of pain, a feeling of vulnerability, or something you can’t quite name, we understand.

You don’t need to explain it away or apologise for it. You don’t need to feel embarrassed. What matters is that you’re here, and that takes courage.

What this guide is for

This guide explains how we work with anxious patients. It’s designed to help you understand what to expect, feel more in control, and know that your comfort is always a priority.

Inside you’ll find

  • Understanding dental anxiety
  • How to prepare before your appointment
  • What happens during your visit
  • Ways we can help you feel comfortable
  • How to find the clinic and where to park

Understanding Your Experience

Why anxiety happens and why it makes sense

Why do I feel so anxious?

A question we get asked so often, and something that we believe understanding is key to helping you move forwards. The most important thing to understand is there is no shame in feeling anxious, it’s just information.

Your brain is trying to protect you

Anxiety is your brain’s way of keeping you safe. It turns on the same alarm that would protect you from real danger, even when the threat is only imagined.

In the dental chair, that alarm can make your heart race, your jaw tighten, and your thoughts race ahead to the worst that could happen. None of that means you’re weak or overreacting.

What we do differently

We never rush you, and we build your dental care around making you, and your nervous system feel safe from the start. We will never just tell you ‘you’ll be fine’ and we will tailor every aspect of your care to you.

You are not your anxiety

Feeling anxious doesn’t make you a difficult patient, and it doesn’t mean you can’t have good dental care. It just means you deserve to find a gentler way of doing it.

Everything in this guide is designed to give you more control, more information, and more comfort. You’re in the driver’s seat.

Before Your Appointment

How to prepare and what to expect

There’s no pressure to feel ready

You don’t need to feel calm before you arrive. You don’t need to have your anxiety “fixed”. We’ll meet you wherever you are and take things one step at a time.

Sometimes the fear of the unknown can feel huge, and it helps to know what to expect, so here’s a clear picture of how we work.

About the questionnaire

With this welcome pack, you have been sent a short questionnaire. It helps us understand your history, your worries and what would make the appointment easier. It’s an important part of us getting to know you, and tailoring things to your needs.

There are no wrong answers, and nothing is shared without your permission.

What happens when you arrive

You’ll be greeted by Alex on the front desk, you will have some time to settle into reception and have a drink if you’d like one, before Dr Lorna will come and take you to your room. We’ll take plenty of time to talk, answer questions and agree on a plan together before anything else happens.

Once you are comfortable, we will plan to carry out a full new patient assessment, including x-rays where indicated and a clean of your teeth if you wish. If you need extra support, we have nitrous oxide (inhalation sedation) on hand, to assist where appropriate.

Things to help you prepare

  • Tell us what you’re worried about, even if it feels small
  • Remember that the dental clinic is no place to feel shame, there’s no judgement here
  • Bring a friend, partner or family member with you
  • Write down your questions before you come, and ask us to explain anything as many times as you need

During Your Appointment

How you stay in control throughout

You are always in control

Nothing happens without your permission. Throughout your appointment, you can ask questions, request a break, or ask us to stop.

We’ll agree on a simple hand signal before we start, so you can let us know when you need a pause without having to speak.

What to expect

We’ll work in short, clearly explained stages. You’ll know what’s happening, what’s coming next, and how long each part will take.

We’ll check in between stages, and you can ask us to slow down or stop at any point. If we need to pause, we simply pause.

Ways we can help you feel comfortable

  • Noise-cancelling headphones and your own music or TV show if you prefer
  • A weighted blanket and a soft cushion
  • Guided breathing and grounding exercises
  • A break whenever you need one
  • A stop signal we agree on together

If it feels too much

If you need to stop, we stop. There’s no judgement and no penalty for taking a break.

We can finish the rest another day, or spread it over more than one visit. Your comfort always comes before our schedule.

Finding Us

Getting here and where to park

If you’re taking the train

Cronulla station is about a ten-minute walk from the clinic. Follow the main road towards the beach and we’re on the left, just past the library.

It can be helpful to look this up on maps in advance. The street view can give you an idea of where you’re walking, which can help take some of the anxiety away when finding a new place.

If you’re driving

There’s no patient parking at the clinic. A good place to try first is the Croydon Street council car park near Cronulla Library.

Enter from Croydon Street and look for a free two- or four-hour bay. Check the sign where you park and choose a space that allows enough time for your appointment and the walk to and from the clinic.

Need a hand finding us?

Give yourself a little extra time to find a space and walk over, so you can arrive at least 10 minutes early and settle in. If you need assistance on the day, call us the morning of your appointment and we’ll talk you through it.
