Understanding the experience

What is The Anxious Patient Experience™?

The Anxious Patient Experience™ begins with a dedicated 70-minute Anxious New Patient Experience.

This first visit follows a consistent APE protocol and flow that is unique to the programme. Before you arrive, we learn about your anxiety, triggers, previous experiences, communication preferences and what helps you feel more comfortable and in control.

During the appointment, the focus is on understanding you first, creating predictability, agreeing how you would like us to communicate with you, giving you clear control throughout the appointment, and supporting nervous-system regulation.

Your visit may also include an examination, X-rays and other appropriate care (such as a clean) where you feel comfortable enough to proceed.

From there, you receive a personalised treatment plan and ongoing APE care pathway, so future treatment can be adapted around your individual needs, preferences and level of support.

The aim is not to promise that anxiety disappears or to ask you to push through it.

It is to help you understand your response, give you practical tools to regulate it, and create a consistent care pathway that helps anxiety become less of a barrier to receiving dental treatment.

The nervous system

Dental anxiety often starts in the body.

Your nervous system is always checking whether you are safe. At the dentist, many things can tell it you are not:

  • Unfamiliar environments
  • Past experiences
  • Claustrophobia
  • Needles
  • Sounds and sensations
  • Embarrassment
  • Unpredictability

What it can feel like

When your body feels unsafe, you might notice:

  • A racing heart
  • Muscle tension
  • Changes in your breathing
  • Shaking
  • Nausea
  • Tears
  • Freezing
  • The urge to leave or cancel

This is not always a conscious choice. It is your body trying to protect you.

Our approach

We don’t ask you to ignore that response. We work with it.

Being told to “just relax” rarely helps. Instead, we help you understand what is happening, give you practical tools to regulate that response and create an environment where accessing care feels possible.

Who is The Anxious Patient Experience™ for?

The Anxious Patient Experience™ is designed for people whose dental anxiety, fear or previous experiences make attending the dentist or receiving treatment more difficult.

You do not need to have severe dental phobia to benefit from the experience. You may simply notice that your heart races before an appointment, you feel tense in the waiting room, you avoid booking, feel embarrassed or overwhelmed, or wish dental care felt easier.

The Anxious Patient Experience™ is not intended as a standalone pathway for patients with severe dental phobia who are unable to tolerate dental care without significant pharmacological support.

For some patients, inhalation sedation, such as nitrous oxide, intravenous sedation, or referral for treatment under general anaesthetic may be more appropriate.

Our role is to help identify the level of support that is right for you rather than asking you to force yourself through an approach that may not be appropriate.

Dr Lorna Gladwin

Creator of The Anxious Patient Experience™

Lorna created The Anxious Patient Experience™ after seeing how often anxiety, rather than the dentistry itself, becomes the biggest barrier to someone receiving care.

Her approach has always centred around understanding the person before the teeth: what makes them anxious, what helps them feel in control, how they prefer to communicate, and what can make treatment feel more manageable.

Originally from northern England, Lorna graduated from the University of Sheffield in 2019 and has worked across NHS hospitals, private practice and referral settings in both the UK and Sydney. She now practises in the Sutherland Shire, with particular interests in restorative dentistry and endodontics.

The Anxious Patient Experience™ brings together the way she has naturally approached anxious patients throughout her career into a structured pathway designed to help more people access dental care with greater understanding, control and confidence.

Questions

Everything you might want to know before you begin.

Am I suitable for The Anxious Patient Experience™?

If your body tenses, your heart races or you avoid the dentist because of how it makes you feel, this was created for you. It may not be sufficient on its own for everyone. More severe dental phobia or complex cases may need additional support, which we will discuss with you.

What if I haven’t been to the dentist for years?

You are not alone, and there is no judgement here. We start from where you are today.

What if I become overwhelmed or cry?

That’s okay. It’s a natural response, not something to apologise for. We pause, give you time and only continue when you are ready.

Do I have to have treatment at my first appointment?

No. Your first appointment is about understanding you and what you need. Any treatment is discussed and planned with you, at your pace.

Can I bring someone with me?

Yes. You’re welcome to bring someone you trust.

Can I use headphones or choose what I watch or listen to?

Yes. We have sound-reducing headphones ready for APE patients, along with weighted blankets if these help you feel more comfortable.

We’ll also ask about your music and TV preferences before your appointment so the room can be prepared for you where possible.

Is sedation available?

Yes. Some patients find that additional support such as inhalation sedation, including nitrous oxide, or intravenous sedation makes dental care feel more manageable.

These options can be discussed with you where appropriate.

The aim is not to take support away from you. We also want to help you understand your anxiety and build practical ways to regulate your response, so you have more tools available to you when receiving dental care.

What happens after my first appointment?

We talk through what we learned together and agree on the next steps. You decide what happens next and when.

How much does it cost?

The New Patient Appointment is 70 minutes and costs $350. A $100 deposit is required to secure your booking.

What is the cancellation and deposit policy?

With 48 hours’ notice or more, your deposit can be transferred or refunded. With less than 48 hours’ notice or non-attendance, your deposit may be retained. If anxiety is making it hard to attend, please contact us rather than simply not attending.
