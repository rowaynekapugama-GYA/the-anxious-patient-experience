Meet the team

You deserve to feel heard.

Your experience matters from the very beginning.

Dr Lorna Gladwin

Dentist and Creator of The Anxious Patient Experience™

Lorna created The Anxious Patient Experience™ for people whose bodies react before they have even reached the dental chair.

Originally from northern England, Lorna graduated from the University of Sheffield in 2019. She has worked across NHS hospitals and private practices in the UK and Sydney, including referral care in private settings. Sydney is now home, and she practises in the Sutherland Shire.

Lorna has a special interest in restorative dentistry and endodontics (root canal treatment). Whatever brings you in, her approach starts with understanding you, not just your teeth.
