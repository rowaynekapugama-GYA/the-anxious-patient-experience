# How to use the APE booking dashboard

Web address: **[your website]/admin/** (for now: https://the-anxious-patient-experience.vercel.app/admin/)
Password: GYA will send it to you separately. Please do not share it by email.

## Blocking days when you are away

1. Go to the dashboard and sign in with the password.
2. Click **Availability** on the left (at the top on a phone).
3. Under **Block time off**, choose the **First day** you are away.
4. If you are away for more than one week, also choose the **Last day**.
5. Add a note if you like, for example "Annual leave". Patients never see it.
6. Click **Block these days**. Every APE Wednesday in that period is now closed to online bookings.
7. Check the list underneath: those days now say **Blocked**.
8. To open a day again, click **Open this day** next to it. To block or open just one time, tap that time.

23 and 30 December 2026 are already blocked for Christmas.

If someone has already booked a day you block, the dashboard tells you their name so you can contact them. Blocking
does not cancel their booking. Cancel it under **Bookings** once you have spoken to them.

## When a booking request comes in

1. You get an email at the APE inbox, and the request shows under **Bookings** as "To confirm". The time is held, so
   nobody else can book it online.
2. Block the time in Core Practice and enter the patient.
3. Reply to the patient to confirm and send the new patient information pack.
4. Click **Mark confirmed**. When the deposit arrives, click **Deposit received**.

If a patient cancels, click **Cancel booking**. The time opens up on the website again.

## Opening special

The **Opening special** page shows how many of the six $250 places have been used. It switches itself off once six
are booked or the special dates have passed. You do not need to do anything.
