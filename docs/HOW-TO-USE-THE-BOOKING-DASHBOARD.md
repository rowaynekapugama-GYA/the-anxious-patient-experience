# How to use the APE booking dashboard

Updated 24 November 2026 (website v1.16.0). Web address: **https://theanxiouspatientexperience.com.au/admin/**.
Each person has their own login. Please never share a password by email.

## When a booking comes in

1. You get an email at the APE inbox, and the booking shows under **Bookings**. With online payment, patients pay the
   $100 deposit while booking, and the booking arrives once it is paid. While they pay it shows as **Paying now**;
   if they don't finish within 30 minutes the time is released and shows under Cancelled as **Released: not paid**.
2. Block the time in Core Practice and enter the patient.
3. Press **Mark confirmed**. The website emails the patient the welcome pack (Lorna's Patient Guide, the printable
   questionnaire and the link to the online questionnaire).

If a patient cancels, press **Cancel booking**. The time opens up on the website again. The website does not email
the patient about a cancellation; please tell them yourself.

## Notes on a booking (new in v1.16.0)

Click the patient's name in Bookings to open the booking. Under **Notes (team only)**:

- **Quick notes:** one tap adds "Called, no answer", "Left a voicemail", "Sent a text" or "Spoke to the patient",
  with your name and the time.
- **A note of your own:** type it in the box and press **Add note** (up to 2,000 characters).
- Every note shows who wrote it and when (Sydney time), newest first.
- **Edit** or **Delete** your own notes. Administrators can delete any note. Deleting asks first.
- In the Bookings list, under the patient's name, you see how many notes there are and the first line of the latest
  one.

Notes are only ever seen by people signed in to the dashboard. They are never sent to the patient, never in an email,
and not in the Excel download. Keep them to call attempts and admin: **please keep clinical details in Core
Practice.** A patient can ask to see what we hold about them, and that includes these notes.

## Onboarding call (new in v1.16.0)

- Open the booking and press **Mark onboarding call completed** once the call is done. It shows who ticked it and
  when, and adds a line to the notes.
- **Undo** puts it back (it asks first, and also adds a line to the notes).
- In the Bookings list each booking that is going ahead shows **Onboarding call: to do** or **Onboarding call: done**.
- The **Onboarding call to do** tab lists only the bookings still to call: upcoming, not cancelled or released, and
  not still paying the deposit.
- Ticking it changes nothing else: no email, no change to the booking, the deposit or the welcome pack.

## What the questionnaire status means

| It says | What happened |
|---|---|
| Questionnaire: not sent yet | The welcome pack (which carries the questionnaire) has not been emailed yet. |
| Questionnaire: sent, not yet completed | The welcome pack has gone to the patient. They have not sent the online questionnaire back. |
| Questionnaire: started, not yet completed | They have opened it and saved some answers, but not pressed Send. |
| Questionnaire: completed 24 Nov | They sent it in on that date. Open it under **Questionnaires** (if your login has access). |
| Questionnaire: not completed, link expired | The link stopped working (a week after the appointment) without it being sent in. |

A patient who fills in the **paper** copy and brings it along still shows as "sent, not yet completed" (or "link
expired" after the appointment), because the website only knows about the online questionnaire.

## Blocking days when you are away

1. Click **Availability**.
2. Under **Block time off**, choose the **First day** you are away (and the **Last day** if it is more than one week).
3. Add a note if you like, for example "Annual leave". Patients never see it.
4. Click **Block these days**. To open a day again, click **Open this day** next to it. To block or open just one
   time, tap that time.

If someone has already booked a day you block, the dashboard tells you their name so you can contact them. Blocking
does not cancel their booking.

## Opening special

The **Opening special** page shows how many of the six $250 places have been used. It switches itself off once six are
booked or the special dates have passed.
