# Treatment plans: a guide for Lorna, Alex and the APE team

Updated 27 November 2026.

## Who can use them

Every dashboard login can create and view treatment plans, including anyone added later. Adding a user is therefore
a clinical access decision. To stop someone seeing plans, untick "Can create and view treatment plans" on the Users
screen. Every view, change, send and download is recorded in the audit log.

## Start a plan

1. Open **Bookings**, click the patient's name, and press **Create treatment plan** under Treatment plans. You can
   pick a template next to the button first.
2. Or go to **Treatment plans**, press **New plan from a booking**, choose the patient, then choose what to
   **Start from**:
   - a blank plan;
   - one of your templates;
   - an earlier plan, which keeps its choices, tick boxes and added paragraphs. Everything typed for that patient is
     cleared.

The patient's name, date of birth, booking reference and appointment come from the booking. Nothing is typed twice.

## Write it

- The plan saves itself a couple of seconds after you stop typing.
- In any section, **Add a paragraph from your library** drops in one of your standard paragraphs. You can then change
  it for this patient.
- **Insert from your library** under a long-text box does the same inside that box.
- These words are filled in for you:
  - `{first_name}`: the patient's first name;
  - `{appointment_date}`: the appointment date;
  - `{dentist}`: the clinician on the plan.
- A yellow box appears if some wording cannot be used: claims that something will not hurt, promises of a result,
  or comparisons with other dentists. Sedation wording also needs the line about talking through how it works and its
  risks somewhere on the plan. The plan cannot be finalised until the wording is changed.

## Finalise, print, download, send

- **Finalise** locks the plan, with the date and your name. **Change this plan** starts version 2 and keeps
  version 1 as it was.
- **Download PDF** saves a practice copy to put into Core Practice.
  - The file is named surname, first name, "treatment plan", the date and the version.
  - The PDF says it is a practice copy, when it was downloaded and by whom.
- **Print** prints the same practice copy.
- **The downloaded file is an unencrypted copy of health information.** Put it into Core Practice, then delete it from
  the Downloads folder. Never leave it on a shared computer.
- **Send to patient** emails the patient a secure link that asks for their date of birth before the plan opens.
  - The email says only that a plan is ready.
  - The patient never gets the plan as an attachment.
  - **Revoke access** stops the link straight away.

## Templates and paragraphs

Go to **Treatment plans > Templates and paragraphs**.

- **New template** lets you set up the wording for a kind of plan, for example a first visit with inhalation
  sedation. **Save as a template** on any plan does the same from a plan you have written. Dates are never kept, and
  nothing about the patient is ever part of a template.
- **Edit**, **Retire** and **Restore** manage templates and paragraphs. Retiring hides one without deleting it.
- The paragraph library started with the wording on your form. **Add a paragraph** adds your own.

## Practice details

At the bottom of every plan (the link page, the PDF and the print) are:

- The Anxious Patient Experience and its email;
- The Cronulla Dentists, its address and its email.

You can change these in **Treatment plans > Settings > Practice details**.
