# Changing the wording of your patient emails

Since 23 November 2026 the emails patients receive are branded, and you can change their wording yourself.

## Where to find it

Sign in to the dashboard, then choose **Patient emails** in the menu. There are three emails:

1. **Booking request received:** goes as soon as a patient books (with online payment, once the deposit is paid).
2. **Appointment confirmed (welcome pack):** goes when you press **Mark confirmed**, with the Patient Guide and the
   questionnaire attached.
3. **Deposit received:** goes when a patient pays their deposit later, from the pay link in their email.

Click an email to open it. The wording is on the left, and a preview with sample details is on the right (Desktop,
Mobile or Plain text).

## What you can change

- **Subject:** what patients see in their inbox.
- **Preheader:** the short line some inboxes show after the subject.
- **Photo:** one of four photos, or none.
- **Eyebrow and heading:** the small line in capitals and the large heading.
- **Main message and second message:**
  - a blank line starts a new paragraph;
  - a line starting with "- " is a bullet point;
  - the buttons above each box add bold, italic, a link or a list.
- **Button:** the words on it and where it goes (the questionnaire link, Book Now, the APE homepage or emailing you).
- **Sign-off and sender name.**

## Merge fields

The grey chips such as `{first_name}` or `{appointment_date}` are filled in for each patient. Click one to put it
where your cursor is. Each email only offers the fields it has information for. In the welcome email, `{where_to_go}`
on a line of its own adds the address, map link, train and parking box.

## Check, test, save

- The preview updates as you type.
- **Send me a test** emails the version you are looking at to your own address only, with [TEST] in the subject.
- **Save** puts it in use for the next email straight away (no Publish needed).
- If something needs fixing, a red box explains it and nothing is saved. Examples:
  - a misspelt merge field, such as `{firstname}`;
  - removing the questionnaire link from the welcome email.
- Words like pain-free, guaranteed, best or expert bring up a reminder about AHPRA's advertising rules. You can still
  save.
- **Version history** keeps the last 10 versions; **Restore** brings one back.
- **Reset to default** goes back to the original wording.

## What you can't change here

- **The appointment details box,** the deposit line and the under-18 line.
- **The deposit policy line.** It always matches your approved policy in Site Settings > Deposit policy.
- **The questionnaire paragraph and "Where to go".**
- **Team emails** (new booking, questionnaire received) and **login emails** (invites and password resets). Their
  wording is fixed.

Never put health details in an email. Questionnaire answers and patients' notes are never included.
